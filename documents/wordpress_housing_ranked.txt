By Lauryn Nakamura

UC Irvine has a wide variety of on campus housing options including dorms, themed houses, graduate & family housing, and five apartment communities provided by UCI’s third-party partner, American Campus Communities (ACC). If you are a second year undergraduate student or higher, this ranking is for you. For this ranking, I will not include Mesa Court and Middle Earth as those are primarily reserved for freshman students. I also will not include the Las Lomas apartments or any of the graduate and family housing options as this will strictly focus on the primary undergraduate housing options. All of the options that I have listed have free WiFi, full kitchens, study rooms, come fully furnished, and have access to the UCI bus service. While it is fair to say that all UCI on campus housing options are great, each community has its own pros and cons. That being said, I have ranked each housing community according to the following categories: distance from campus, amenities, price, floor plan options, and spaces provided. This review provides information from myself, current/past residents, and additional UCI students who have been to these housing communities. At the end of the day, it is up to personal preference.

5. Arroyo Vista

Distance: About 15-25 Minute Walk

Amenities:

Pool: No

Laundry: Separate

Price: $925 (Double Room) or $1,121 (Double Suite)

Floor Plans Options: 3 (8, 12, or 16 Bedroom)

Spaces Provided: 42 Total Houses & 1,046 Total Spaces

The Arroyo Vista theme houses are by far the cheapest option on average. With two main bedroom layouts and three floorplan options, residents of Arroyo Vista (with the exception of the Resident Assistants) share a room with another student and share the house with up to 31 other students. Themes of the houses include sororities & fraternities, transfer students, international students, humanities, outdoor adventure, biology, STEM, culinary, and many more. Arroyo Vista is well known for its community aspect as students are encouraged to participate in house activities based on their house’s theme. However, what the UCI housing website will not tell you is that many students experience flooding and ant infestations. While many other previous and current Arroyo Vista residents have told me about these issues, I have also experienced this first hand myself. See videos of my house ceiling leaking here. While myself and many others were able to bond with their housemates and experience a great sense of community, the lack of floor plan options, requirement to have a roommate, lack of amenities, and lack of consistent amenities that are up to date places AV at the bottom of this list.

4. Puerta del Sol

Distance: About 10-15 Minute Walk

Amenities:

Pool: NO

Laundry: Separate & Included

Access to All Other ACC Amenities

Price: $799-$1,810

Floor Plans Options: 5

Spaces Provided: 389 Units & 565 Beds

Puerta del Sol is the closest ACC apartment to campus and the closest of all the undergraduate options on this list. As opposed to three out of five of the ACC apartment communities, Puerta del Sol is one of the two that does not have its own pool. However, as a resident of the ACC apartment communities, residents of Puerta del Sol have access to the other ACC apartment amenities and can use the other pools and gyms provided by ACC. With five different floorplan options, residents of Puerta del Sol have the option to live by themselves or share a room with another student. While the options for living by yourself can be great, what the ACC Puerta del Sol housing website will not tell you is that many of the students who have to share a room struggle with their living spaces. Third year student Celia Levin is a current resident of Puerta del Sol and shares a room. She explains that while having free laundry and a close walk to campus is nice, she has to share a closet with two people and there are no desks in your room, so you have to bring your own. Another third year Puerta del Sol resident, Noel Vu also informed me that living in Puerta can make her feel like there is no privacy because you are right on the street where many other students walk to and from class and people keep looking into her window. In spite of the close distance to campus, the lack of space, units available, and floor plan options,  places this community at number four.

3. Vista del Campo & Vista del Campo Norte

Distance: about 20-40 minute walk

Amenities:

Pool: YES

Laundry: Separate

Access to All Other ACC Amenities

Price: $884-$1,794

Floor Plans Options: 4 (VDC) and 4 (VDCN)

VDC Spaces Provided: 488 Units & 1,488 Beds

VDCN Spaces Provided:  545 Units & 1,564 Beds

For this ranking, I have decided to combine Vista del Campo and Vista del Campo Norte as there are not many vast differences between the two communities in regards to amenities and student reviews. Both Vista del Campo and Vista del Campo Norte have access to a pool, hottub, community center and gym. However,Vista del Campo is known for being the farthest housing community at UCI with the farthest walk being well over 30 minutes. Some students in Vista del Campo and Vista del Campo Norte also complain about the WiFi not being great. Furthermore, many mention that in comparison to the cleanliness and furniture that they have seen at their friend’s units in Plaza Verde, Vista del Campo and Vista del Campo Norte can feel out of date. Due to the variety in housing options, level of amenities, and distance from campus, these two housing communities are tied for third place.

2. Plaza Verde/Plaza Verde II

Distance: about 15-25 minute walk

Amenities:

Pool: NO

Laundry: Separate

Access to All Other ACC Amenities

Price: $819-$1,697

Floor Plans Options: 6

PV Spaces Provided: 410 Units & 1,441 Beds

PV II Spaces Provided:  295 Units & 1,072 Beds

Similarly to VDC and VDCN, there is not a large difference between Plaza Verde and Plaza Verde II in regards to amenities. However, as opposed to VDC and VDCN, Plaza Verde has the same floor plan options as Plaza Verde II. Plaza Verde and Plaza Verde II are ACC’s new additions to UCI’s housing communities. Available to students for the first time this past 2023-2024 school year, Plaza Verde II has the newest amenities with Plaza Verde being its slightly older sibling. The community center and study rooms are brand new with pool tables, ping pong, and flat screen TVs in the lobby. Plaza Verde and Plaza Verde II both do not have their own pools, but they are each a 10-15 minute walk to the nearest ACC pool as opposed to Puerta del Sol which would be a 15-20 minute walk. Some Plaza Verde residents have also mentioned that in spite of having to share a room, they have their own desks and space within their shared room. While you may have a roommate in one of the Plaza Verde communities, the clean/brand new amenities and walking distance to campus or the nearest pool, make these two housing communities tied for the number two spot on this list.

1. Camino del Sol

Distance: about 20-30 minute walk

Amenities:

Pool: YES

Laundry: Included

Access to All Other ACC Amenities

Price: $1,103-$1,462

Amount of Floor Plans: 6

Spaces Provided: 331 units & 1,198 beds

And the grand prize goes to… Camino del Sol! Camino del Sol takes the top spot on this list as it is not too far from campus, has in-unit laundry, and you guessed it… a pool and hot tub! Many students will host club events at the outdoor kitchen that Camino offers, similarly to the two other ACC communities that also have pool/hot tub. Located on the east end of campus, Camino del Sol has the perfect balance between newer amenities, distance, and you do not have to share a room! There are very few complaints about Camino as the main ones regard parking as the only available reserved parking is at the Anteater Recreation Center parking lot (same as Puerta del Sol). Camino del Sol is also one of the closest housing communities to the Anteater Recreation Center as opposed to Puerta del Sol. Furthermore, all Camino del Sol floor plans are not shared and students only live with up to three other students. While taking into account all of the categories: distance, amenities, price, floor plans, and units available, I recommend Camino del Sol as your top choice for undergraduate housing.
